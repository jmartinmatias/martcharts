Taller

elBulli's creative method, applied to charts. Pick a dish: its ingredients (the data and its fields) stay exactly as they are, while the kitchen varies the technique, the tools, the temperature, the texture and the plating. Every plate is scored on five tastes and served only when it is balanced, valid and new.
